How to play
① Summon servants with gold. The gacha starts at E-grade — spend ✧ spirit to upgrade it through D·C·B·A·S.
② Select a servant on the bench, click a platform to deploy. Population is limited!
③ Combine servants by recipe to ascend the grade ladder E→D→C→B→A→S→EX→★. Open the ✦ Codex for all 177 recipes.
④ Earn ❋ wisps each wave; convert them to gold, spirit, or free summons. Release servants to recover wisps.
⑤ Choose a Faction A–E for a unique passive + active. Faction XP persists between runs. Try Gold / Servant Gambling if you dare.
⑥ Enemies march the ring 1½ laps. Survive every wave (bosses every 5). Choose your difficulty — up to 100 waves.
Keys: Space = wave · G = summon · P = pause · Esc = deselect
